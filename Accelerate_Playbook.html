The Engineering Leader's Playbook

An interactive capabilities audit for engineering managers, based on the research findings of Accelerate: The Science of Lean Software and DevOps. Use this tool to evaluate your organization, identify systemic gaps, and drive capability growth.

Want to read the source material? Dive into accelerate.md.
Chapter 1

Focus on Capabilities, Not Maturity

Maturity models focus on static, lock-step templates that prescribe the same path for all teams, aiming for a finished state. Capability models are dynamic, contextual, and outcome-oriented, allowing custom roadmaps for continuous improvement.

Chapter 2

Measuring Delivery Performance

Software delivery performance is rigorously captured by four key metrics. Crucially, the best performers achieve speed and reliability simultaneously, proving there is no trade-off between the two.

Chapter 3

Culture & Collaboration

Sociologist Ron Westrum's typology classifies cultures into Pathological, Bureaucratic, and Generative. A Generative culture predicts higher performance and lower burnout. You can act your way into a generative culture by adopting technical and Lean practices.
Manager Investigations
Chapter 4

Continuous Integration & Delivery

Technical practices predict continuous delivery. Version control of everything (code, configuration, environment setup), automated builds, automated test suites, and daily trunk merging are critical indicators of flow.
Manager Investigations
Chapter 5

Loosely Coupled Architecture

The specific technology stack matters less than its design. Loose architectural coupling enables teams to test, change, and deploy services independently, allowing developer productivity to scale linearly with team growth.
Manager Investigations
Chapter 6

Shifting Security Left

Integrating security directly into the daily software lifecycle—rather than checking it manually at the end—speeds up delivery and increases overall stability.
Manager Investigations
Chapter 7

Lean Management Practices

Lean management scales delivery by minimizing governance theater. External change approval boards (CABs) are negatively correlated with performance. Visualizing work, limiting WIP, and using monitoring systems proactively drive success.
Manager Investigations
Chapter 8

Lean Product Development

Working in small batches enables short lead times and fast feedback. Product success requires slicing features, gathering user feedback, and empowering teams to run experiments independently.
Manager Investigations
Chapter 9

Preventing Burnout

Burnout is organizational exhaustion caused by stress. Managers must prevent it by fostering supportive environments, ensuring values alignment, and improving continuous delivery systems to reduce daily friction.
Manager Investigations
Chapter 10

Satisfaction & Employee NPS

Job satisfaction and employee loyalty (eNPS) directly affect organizational retention and performance. Satisfaction depends on giving developers effective tools and utilizing their skills.
Manager Investigations
Chapter 11

Transformational Leadership

Effective leaders guide, support, and intellectually stimulate their teams. Leaders do not directly implement changes, but they enable the capabilities, resources, and trust needed to succeed.
